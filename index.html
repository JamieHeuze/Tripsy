Practical Notes

🗺
Lunch stop approach
Park once, stroll, lunch, continue. Keep each lunch stop to 60–90 minutes to stay comfortable and arrive at hotels by early evening.
🌿
Pace guidance
This trip is designed for a calm, pregnancy-friendly pace. No back-to-back sightseeing. Prioritise comfort, good food, and scenery over ticking boxes.
💆‍♀️
Lefay spa bookings
Pre-book spa treatments at Lefay Resort in advance — the wellness facilities are the centrepiece of the Lake Garda stay. Book at time of hotel confirmation if possible.
🍋
Garda day trips
Limone sul Garda and Salò are both under 30 minutes from Gargnano. Neither requires more than a short stroll and a long lunch. Keep effort low.
🛣
Driving style
All driving legs are 3–5 hours including stops. Never push through — leave time to enjoy the scenery and stop if tired. Mont Blanc tunnel on Day 3 is the scenic highlight.
🌙
Final day
Keep Day 10 completely relaxed. Barive to Calais is only 3 hours. Leave when ready, stop for coffee if you want, arrive with time to spare before the crossing.
🥂
Food philosophy
Prioritise one long, wonderful meal per day over snacking. Lunch stops have been chosen for quality — Beaune, Aosta, Ascona, and Colmar are all genuinely excellent.
