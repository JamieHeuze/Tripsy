Practical notes

🗺
Lunch stop approach
Park once, stroll, lunch, continue. Keep each stop to 60–90 minutes to stay comfortable and arrive at hotels by early evening.
🌿
Pace guidance
This trip is designed for a calm, pregnancy-friendly pace. No back-to-back sightseeing. Prioritise comfort, good food, and scenery over ticking boxes.
💆
Lefay spa — pre-book
Pre-book spa treatments at Lefay Resort in advance. The wellness facilities are the centrepiece of the Lake Garda stay — book at time of hotel confirmation.
🍋
Garda day trips
Limone sul Garda and Salò are both under 30 minutes from Gargnano. Neither requires more than a short stroll and a long lunch. Keep effort low.
🚗
Driving philosophy
All legs are 3–5 hours including stops. Never push through — leave time to enjoy the scenery. The Mont Blanc tunnel on Day 3 is the scenic highlight of the drive.
🌙
Final day — no rush
Day 10 is only 3 hours, Barive to Calais. Leave when ready, stop for coffee if you want, arrive with time to spare before the crossing.
🥂
Food philosophy
Prioritise one long, wonderful meal per day. All four lunch stops — Beaune, Aosta, Ascona, Colmar — are genuinely excellent choices.
